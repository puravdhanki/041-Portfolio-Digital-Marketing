Operations & Content Coordination

Scaling an 80+ Member Content Production Pipeline at PulseX Media

The Operational Challenge

PulseX Media Solutions faced rapidly expanding client deliverables across short-form videos (Reels, TikToks, Shorts) and long-form YouTube editing. With an 80+ member workforce, communication gaps, inconsistent video rendering checks, and missed turnaround windows risked client retention.

Strategic Interventions & Execution

  • Tiered Pod Architecture: Divided the 80+ team into agile 10-person pods, appointing lead quality reviewers for pre-delivery inspection.
  • Standardized Quality Assurance (QA) Checklist: Implemented a 12-point QA protocol covering audio sync, hook pacing, color grade consistency, caption accuracy, and aspect ratio compliance.
  • Daily Sprints & Bottleneck Identification: Instituted daily 15-minute syncs to identify asset delays early, allowing dynamic task re-allocation before deadline compromises occurred.
  • Executive Feedback Loop: Acted as the vital operational bridge between executive management and frontline editors to refine delivery expectations and improve morale.

Key Results & Impact

-45% Client Revision Cycles
